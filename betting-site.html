Help & FAQ — TA7

Aam sawalon ke jawab. Ye poori site demo hai — koi asli paisa nahi.

Ye site asli betting hai ya demo?

Sirf demo. Tamam credits virtual hain, in ki koi cash value nahi, na deposit hota hai na withdrawal. Real-money betting ke liye licence chahiye jo abhi nahi hai.

Demo credits khatam ho jayen to?

Wallet page se kabhi bhi free top-up (+500 / +1,000 / +5,000) ya roz ka Daily Bonus +1,000 claim kar lo. Koi payment nahi chahiye.

Single, Multiple aur System bet me kya farq hai?

Single: har selection alag bet. Multiple/Acca: sab selections ek bet me, odds multiply hote hain, sab jeetna zaroori hai (3+ par +10% demo boost). System: combinations banti hain (maslan 2/3) — ek selection haar bhi jaye to kuch combinations jeet sakti hain.

Bet cancel kab ho sakti hai?

Sirf tab jab match abhi shuru nahi hua (Upcoming). My Bets page me Pending bet par Cancel button se poora stake wapas wallet me aa jata hai. Live match ki bet cancel nahi hoti — us par Cash Out demo hai.

Cash Out (Full / Partial) kaise kaam karta hai?

Pending bet par Cash Out dabao: Full se poori bet demo value par settle ho jati hai; Partial (50%) se aadhi value milti hai aur baqi bet aadhe stake par chalti rehti hai. Value live odds ke hisaab se demo me badalti hai.

Live odds kyun badalte hain?

Live/In-Play matches me demo odds har kuch seconds me score ke hisaab se upar-neeche hote hain — bilkul asli in-play betting ki practice ke liye.

Deposit Settings khali kyun hain?

Easypaisa / JazzCash / Other Bank fields jaan kar bilkul blank rakhe gaye hain aur Deposit button disabled hai. Jab tak licence nahi milta koi real-money deposit nahi hoga — sirf demo credits chalenge.

Demo limit kaise set karun?

Limits & Settings page se per-bet aur daily demo limit lagao. Limit se zyada stake place nahi hoga. 18+ warning hamesha on rehti hai.

Result kaise settle hota hai?

Demo me My Bets page ka “Simulate Results” button pending bets ko demo result se settle karta hai. Koi result rigged nahi hai — ye sirf practice flow hai, kisi ki bet jaan kar harwayi/jitwayi nahi jati.
